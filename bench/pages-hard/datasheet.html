5.1 Typical Application
The circuit in Figure 5-2 uses one channel of the LPV2192 as a single-supply, noninverting amplifier for a bridge or thermocouple front end. Gain is set by RF and RG (G = 1 + RF / RG = 11 V/V). Place the 0.1-µF ceramic capacitor as close to the V+ pin as possible; a 10-µF bulk capacitor shared between devices is recommended for long supply traces. Unused channel B is configured as a grounded buffer.
